Our privacy promise, in plain language

We only ever see a site's name, never your history, never the page. The live check sends the hostname of the page you visit to exactly one endpoint, graph.whisper.online, and nothing else: no path, no query, no content. Hostnames are not retained to build a browsing profile. The on-device look-alike protection sends nothing at all, and one switch in settings turns the live check off entirely.

Your browser may say Whisper Guard can "read your browsing activity". That permission is how we see the site name to check it. We never read the page, never see what you type, and never store your history. There is no telemetry and no analytics in this extension.

What signing in adds (free)

With an account: your whole fleet in one view (every device and agent on your Whisper account), per-endpoint drill-downs with verifiable identity health, protection presets that show you what they would have blocked on your own traffic, and one control that gives this browser its own routable Whisper IPv6 address and routes its traffic through it, so it becomes a first-class endpoint that anyone can verify by RDAP.

You can revisit all of this any time in the extension's settings.
